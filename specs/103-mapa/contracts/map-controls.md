# Contract: Map controls

**Feature**: `103-mapa`

## Panel

- Position: bottom-right over map stage
- Actions: zoom in, zoom out, reset 1:1 (`resetTransform`), focus group
- Style: translucent surface + readable icons (both app themes, light/dark map art)
- Focus group: disabled or omitted when no `grupo`
- Replace map (edit mode): may remain in same stack; not part of lore legend

## i18n

All aria-labels / titles in pt-BR and en (`mapa` namespace).
